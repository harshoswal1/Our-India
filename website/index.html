Who runs your area, and what needs fixing in it.

Our India shows who leads each political party from the national office down to your district, every name linked to its source. Inside it, NagrikVoice lets you photograph a civic problem and put it on a public record until it's fixed.

Two halves, one app

Leaders

Party organisations, mapped

25 parties, from national president to district units. Explore them as an orbit, a classic chart, a map of India, or charts of where each party is organised.

  • Every named post links to the article it was checked against
  • Posts we couldn't confirm say "Not yet recorded" instead of guessing
  • Use your location to jump to the heads for your state and district
NagrikVoice

Civic problems, on the record

A feed of potholes, broken lights, leaks and more, reported by people with a photo taken on the spot. Support a report to raise its priority; it stays open until a fix is verified.

  • Live in 48 municipal corporations and councils
  • Your GPS picks the corporation the report goes to
  • City ranking by unresolved, delivered complaints

Reporting a problem takes a minute

  1. Photograph itOn the spot, with the camera. Gallery photos aren't accepted as evidence.
  2. Confirm the placeLocation fills itself in and names the corporation responsible.
  3. Say what's wrongPick a department, add a title. If someone already reported the same spot, support theirs instead.
  4. Follow itDelivered, open, overdue, fix under review, resolved: every change is on the public record.

How we treat the data

Sourced, not scraped blind

Each party post was checked against a published report: party sites, Wikipedia, national and regional news.

Re-checked monthly

National and state heads are compared with Wikipedia every month. A change shows up straight away, marked as not yet verified, with a link.

Honest about limits

Email delivery to corporations is still simulated while the mail service is set up, and the app labels those reports "demo delivery".

Download

The app isn't on the Play Store yet. Install the APK directly:

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Our India, follow the short tour, and allow location.
